TePMA — where to apply in India

Offline voice kiosk · Indian-language resume & document generation · fully local inference

Compiled 19 August 2026 · deadlines verified against official / press sources · green = strong fit, amber = partial fit, grey = weak or closed

Read this first. Most Indian government grants below require DPIIT Startup Recognition, which in turn needs a registered Pvt Ltd / LLP / partnership. It is free, online, and usually issued in days. If TePMA is not yet incorporated, that single step is what unlocks SISFS, National Startup Awards, and most state schemes — do it before the September deadlines. The EiR-type programmes (GENESIS, TIDE) are the exception: they are built for individuals who have not incorporated yet.

MeitY Startup Hub, via empanelled incubators
Rolling · cohort closes ~31 Aug
₹4–10 lakh equity-free, routed through your chosen incubator

BEST FIT This is the closest thing to a programme designed for exactly your situation: an individual with a working prototype and no company yet. It is an ICT/AI scheme under MeitY, explicitly weighted toward Tier-II/III city impact — which is TePMA's whole argument. You apply through an MSH-empanelled incubator; pick one near you and apply to that incubator's call directly.

What to lead with: the offline constraint. MeitY funds digital-inclusion infrastructure, and "works with no internet, no cloud, no data leaving the box" is a policy story, not just an engineering one.

Not grants — where the kiosk actually goes

Funding is the easier half. For a kiosk, the harder and more valuable question is who owns the floor space. These are the distribution channels worth approaching in parallel — a signed pilot with any one of them makes every grant application above dramatically stronger.
  • CSC (Common Services Centres) — CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd operates a network of village-level kiosks already delivering citizen services, run by local Village Level Entrepreneurs. This is the single most natural home for TePMA. Approach as a service to be onboarded onto the CSC platform.
  • National Career Service (NCS) — Directorate General of Employment, Ministry of Labour. Model Career Centres already offer resume assistance to jobseekers, mostly manually. TePMA automates their existing job.
  • NSDC / Skill India centres — training centres whose graduates all need resumes at exactly the same moment.
  • State employment exchanges and district libraries — lower procurement bar than central government, good for a first paid pilot.
  • Rojgar Melas / job fairs — high-density, time-boxed demand; a strong venue for proving throughput numbers you can cite in applications.

Suggested order of operations

  • This week — register with Bhashini as a startup partner; email the VYOMA team a demo video. Both free, both compounding.
  • This month — apply to GENESIS EiR through a nearby MSH incubator, and to a PRAYAS Centre. These are the two that accept you as an individual with a prototype.
  • If a student — get on an SIH 2026 team before your college's internal deadline.
  • Next 60 days — incorporate and get DPIIT recognition. It gates SISFS, National Startup Awards, and most state schemes.
  • In parallel, always — chase one CSC or NCS pilot. Evidence of real users beats every deck.
